=== Suraksha Laghu Evan Simant Vriddhajan Krishak Samman Pension Yojana (65e16ef6da55878ebe0a7eea) ===
--- eligibility ---
Suraksha Laghu Evan Simant Vriddhajan Krishak Samman Pension Yojana — Eligibility: 1. The applicant should be a native of Rajasthan State.
1. The applicant should be a farmer.
1. The female applicant&#39;s age should be more than 55 years and the male more than 58 years.
--- benefits ---
Suraksha Laghu Evan Simant Vriddhajan Krishak Samman Pension Yojana — Benefits: 1. To a pensioner below 75 years of age : ₹750/- per month
1. To pensioners aged 75 years and above : ₹1,000/- per month

=== Mukhyamantri Vriddhjan Samman Pension Yojana (65df6083da55878ebe07fa24) ===
--- eligibility ---
Mukhyamantri Vriddhjan Samman Pension Yojana — Eligibility: 1. The applicant should be a native of Rajasthan and living in Rajasthan,
1. The female applicant should be 55 years or older.
1.  The male applicant should be aged 58 years or older
1. The total annual income of the wife/husband from all sources should be less than ₹48,000/-
1. People belonging to BPL/Antyodaya/Aastha Card holding families/Sahariya/Kathauri, Khairwa castes have been exempted from the income-related condition.
--- benefits ---
Mukhyamantri Vriddhjan Samman Pension Yojana — Benefits: > **Pension amount : ₹1,000/- per month**
